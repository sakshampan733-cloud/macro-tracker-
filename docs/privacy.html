BASAL

Privacy

Last updated 20 August 2026

Basal is a personal project with one user. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no company behind it. Nothing you log is collected, sold, or seen by anyone else — because there is nowhere for it to go.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — meals, weight, water, supplements, blood markers, settings — is stored in your own browser, on your own device, using local storage. It is never uploaded to any server operated by this project, because no such server exists.

If you clear your browser data, it is gone. That is the trade-off of keeping it entirely local, which is why the app has an export button.

Whoop

If you connect Whoop, the app reads your recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and strain in order to show them alongside your food log and adjust your daily calorie target.

Whoop's API cannot be called directly by a web page, so requests pass through a small relay that you deploy and control. The relay:

  • holds only the Whoop application credentials, which cannot be published in a web page;
  • forwards requests to Whoop and returns the response;
  • stores nothing — no health data, no tokens, no logs of your activity.

Your Whoop access tokens are returned to your device and stored there, with the rest of your data. You can disconnect at any time, which deletes them, and revoke access from your Whoop account settings.

Food lookups

When you scan or type a barcode, the digits are sent to Open Food Facts, a public non-profit food database, to fetch that product's nutrition information. Only the barcode number is sent. Nothing about you, your log, or your device is included.

What is never collected

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No cookies for tracking or advertising
  • No third-party scripts — the app has no external dependencies at runtime
  • No location, contacts, or device identifiers
  • No account, email address, or password, because there is no sign-in

Camera

Barcode scanning uses your camera to read the code. Frames are processed on your device and discarded immediately. No image is stored, uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

Your control

  • Export — take a full copy of everything as a JSON file at any time
  • Erase — delete everything from the device in one action
  • Disconnect Whoop — removes stored tokens immediately

Not medical advice

Basal estimates and displays nutrition information. It is not a medical device, it has not been reviewed by any clinician, and nothing it shows is a diagnosis or a treatment recommendation. Decisions about your health belong with a qualified professional.
